## Canonical direction

The article is not really about owning many bottles. It is about a collection that cannot reach a stable ending: curiosity creates expansion, scarcity creates preservation, and the physical shelf becomes evidence of both impulses.

The page should therefore behave like a collection.

> **The composition accumulates while the argument accumulates, then abruptly gives the reader space again.**

This is the first specimen of the new Aromia. It should prove that an article does not enter a fixed visual template: the story itself determines density, image, rhythm and silence.

## Founding Design result

The rejected Canva work is historical evidence only. It is no longer the active design surface.

Claude Design produced a stronger founding specimen in `design-lab/el-coleccionista/` with a repeatable relationship rather than a repeatable layout:

`CARRIL → CAMPO DE LECTURA → ZONA MARGINAL`

The identity comes from the relationship among those zones. The narrative comes from **how much of each zone is occupied**.

Accepted as promising evidence for Aromia:

- three-zone editorial field;
- Newsreader as essay body / editorial voice, Archivo as interface voice, IBM Plex Sans as metadata/data voice;
- caption language based on a top rule rather than cards/boxes;
- intrinsic responsive collapse where marginal notes become interludes;
- archive surface as a change of register;
- restrained contextual close;
- act indicator instead of a generic progress bar;
- absence / empty zones as authored content.

Not automatically Foundation:

- exact `110 → 56 → 36 → 22px` compression curve;
- four exact flanker bands;
- right-side overflow gesture;
- `300px` exact reset;
- title-smaller-than-body relationship in “Sí, pero”;
- Aventus ruler placement;
- any concrete counting sequence belonging to this story.

**Repeat identity. Vary composition.**

## Visual thesis by act

### Act I — Recognition

Quiet and domestic, not luxurious. Near-white paper, editorial typography, generous negative space. The first visual should feel like finding somebody's actual perfume shelf or drawer rather than seeing a campaign.

The reader should recognize the behavior before Aromia explains it.

### Act II — Accumulation

As the argument moves into flankers and continuous multiplication, the page becomes progressively more occupied.

Use density as narrative material:

- increasingly occupied marginal field;
- factual labels only when sourced;
- repetition / seriality;
- tighter vertical cadence;
- broader horizontal pressure;
- authentic product identity only where literal products are shown.

The change must be gradual enough to be felt rather than announced.

### Act III — Fear of disappearance

The emotional register changes from novelty to preservation.

Reduce multiplication and introduce archive/time behavior instead. A timeline or ruler may use Aventus `2010` as a sourced endpoint, but no invented batch chronology, reformulation dates or formula evidence may appear.

### Act IV — “Sí, pero”

Break the accumulation.

Return to a large field of near-white space. Remove most marginal devices. Let the prose carry the section. The absence is the counterargument.

No image is required here. Filling this section would be a regression.

### Act V — Close

The page should close quietly, then allow contextual commercial references only after the editorial ending has already landed.

## Fact-driven visual corrections

The founding prototype must be interpreted against `research/el-coleccionista-fact-check.md`.

### Remove 4/10 household marks

The claim `casi cuatro de cada diez hogares en Estados Unidos coleccionan algo` is not sufficiently supported in that form.

Therefore:

- remove the `4/10` visual from the final composition;
- do not substitute a different number merely to preserve the primitive;
- keep the prose qualitative unless Editorial later supplies a primary source.

This is an important system lesson: **a primitive never gets to manufacture a need for data.**

### Keep 30–40% only as attributed estimate

The `30–40%` flanker figure is usable only as Johanna Monange's estimate for major brands as reported by *Glossy*.

If represented graphically, caption it as an attributed estimate. A `35/100` midpoint may be used as an interpretive mark only if the label makes clear that it visualizes a range rather than pretending `35%` was measured.

Safer default: show the range typographically (`30—40%`) rather than inventing a single exact count.

### Correct dsm-firmenich attribution

Name **Dora Baghriche**. Do not present Aromia's `cada nariz ... es un hueco por llenar` paraphrase as her quote.

### Refresh Le Male family

Use current official examples when literal product names appear: Le Male Le Parfum, Le Male Elixir and Le Male Elixir Absolu are suitable contemporary references. Do not rely on an older lineup solely because the founding prototype used it.

### Aventus

`2010` is verified by Creed and may remain as the starting date of the time device.

Claude Design has discovered structure. **ChatGPT still owns the actual visual composition of this article.**

1. the opening visual relationship inside the three-zone field;
2. the exact accumulation sequence after removal of the unsupported `4/10` graphic;
3. whether the flanker section is literal product identity or abstract/typographic proliferation;
4. the archive / conservation composition;
5. the exact transition into and out of “Sí, pero”;
6. mobile sequencing as its own composition;
7. the final treatment of the `A.` identity mark;
8. which Design primitives remain local versus candidates for later system extraction.

Visual Composition is not filling Design slots. It may replace or reject a proposed visual device if the story, source quality or authenticity requires it.

## Visual opportunity 01 — lived-in collection

**Decision:** ACCEPT, documentary first.

Purpose: recognition.

Preferred asset hierarchy:
1. authentic editorial/documentary photograph of a lived-in mixed perfume collection with appropriate rights;
2. a deliberately constructed/generative scene only if clearly labeled/treated as interpretive and unable to impersonate identifiable product truth;
3. abstraction/no-image if authenticity cannot be protected.

Do not generate pseudo-real branded bottles.

The composition should retain imperfection: different heights, asymmetrical spacing, signs that the shelf exists for use rather than display. Avoid excessive clutter engineered for drama.

## Visual opportunity 02 — flankers multiply

**Decision:** the literal bottle family is no longer mandatory.

The founding Design specimen demonstrates that the article can express proliferation typographically/serially without turning the passage into a product lineup.

Preferred order:
1. story-specific serial field / typographic proliferation, using only verified current names where names appear;
2. authentic verified cutouts only if they materially improve the argument;
3. never a generated family portrait of invented or altered Jean Paul Gaultier bottles.

No prices, retail badges or conversion CTA inside the composition.

## Visual opportunity 03 — conservation / archive

The conservation triptych remains conceptually useful but must not imply factual product history.

Strongest direction:
- three states of **one non-branded or identity-safe perfume object** used as an interpretive preservation study;
- or an authentic real collection photograph where the three states genuinely exist and rights are clear.

If generative, it is explicitly interpretive and cannot use a recognizable branded bottle as documentary evidence.

## Palette — color found in the story

Do not impose legacy black/gold luxury codes.

Foundation candidate:
- warm paper;
- warm ink-black;
- accessible muted brown-gray;
- documentary vitela when archive register is earned.

Episode accent may come from authentic Le Male navy/blue material, but the accent belongs to this article rather than becoming a global Aromia brand color.

No gold is required.

## Typography and hierarchy

The founding specimen corrects the previous assumption about body type:

- `font-display` / Newsreader: title, essay body, major editorial figures;
- `font-sans` / Archivo: interface/navigation/functional close;
- `font-plex` / IBM Plex Sans: captions, metadata, data, marginal notes.

Opening title must have authority without becoming a generic 100vh luxury headline.

## Contextual commerce

Preserve the commercial opportunity but keep it after the editorial close.

**Para quien quiera seguir oliendo**

Two restrained references:
- Le Male — because it is the article's concrete family-expansion example;
- Aventus — because it is the preservation/scarcity example.

Each reference explains *why it appeared in the story* before any action such as `Ver opciones disponibles` or `Encontrarla`.

Affiliate disclosure must remain clear. Amazon is never the visual protagonist.

## Responsive behavior

Mobile is its own composition, not a compressed desktop spread.

- marginalia become inline interludes when appropriate;
- density is expressed through sequence and cadence, not sidebars squeezed into a phone;
- deliberate overflow is allowed only if it remains legible and purposeful;
- “Sí, pero” must visibly reset the accumulated pressure;
- captions remain connected to assets;
- no carousel merely because width is narrow.

## Acceptance gate

Before implementation:

1. Does the page physically become denser as the argument accumulates?
2. Does “Sí, pero” visibly release that density?
3. Does the opening feel lived-in rather than styled for a luxury campaign?
4. Are all documentary-looking products authentic and verifiable?
5. Is any generative imagery clearly interpretive rather than counterfeit documentary evidence?
6. Has unsupported `4/10` data disappeared from final composition?
7. Is the flanker range attribution honest rather than false precision?
8. Does commerce remain subordinate?
9. Does mobile preserve the narrative rhythm?
10. Would the page still be recognizable as this story if the title were hidden?
11. Does the composition demonstrate professional editorial craft?
12. Could the exact arrangement be reused unchanged for the next five articles? If yes, reject as too templated.
