## Narrative behavior
Travel and encounter establish the story; material density gives it weight. Oman must be specific, not a generic luxury-Arabia backdrop.

## Desktop sequence
1. **Opening / 100vh** — series marker `FUERA DEL RADAR`, territory `HISTORIAS`. Title occupies a tall left column. Right field uses authentic Omani architecture/landscape or an interpretive limestone-light threshold. No sultan portrait, crown or bottle.
2. **Arrival** — opening prose crosses into a narrow visual threshold suggesting movement/arrival. A small map or archival travel reference is allowed only if accurate and sourced.
3. **Incense territory** — real frankincense resin/tree/trade imagery becomes the first documentary material anchor. Caption identifies place/material precisely.
4. **Guy Robert** — authentic portrait/archive only if licensable. Otherwise use text and verified career chronology; do not fabricate a historical scene.
5. **Commission / Gold** — Gold Woman/Gold Man authentic imagery may appear here if available. Bottle is evidence of outcome, not hero fantasy.
6. **Material density** — interpretive still life with a deliberately limited set of named materials. Dense crop and smoke/resin behavior imply complexity without pretending to show 140 ingredients.
7. **Most expensive?** — visual tone changes: near-white fact-check interruption. The reputation is presented as reputation, never as a giant factual claim.
8. **Sí, pero** — continue the critical release.
9. **Legacy / today** — contemporary Amouage enters late. Authentic Interlude Man / Reflection Man packshots can accompany restrained commerce/exploration.

## Mobile
Architecture/threshold crop follows title. Documentary frankincense gets priority over decorative imagery. Material still life becomes one strong crop. Contemporary product remains small and late. Preserve the white fact-check interruption.

## Image jobs
A. Omani place/material reference: establish territory truthfully.
B. Documentary frankincense: connect history to matter.

## Restraint
No desert cliché sequence. No crowns, gold bars, palace fantasy, fake calligraphy or invented documentary scene. Deep blue may appear episodically but does not become a generic Amouage-branded page skin.
