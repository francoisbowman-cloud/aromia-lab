# EDITORIAL BATCH 01 — EARLY OMNI REVIEW

GATE: DOCUMENTED_PROXY
RESULT: PASS_WITH_REFINEMENTS

This is an early creative critique performed in the current ChatGPT art-direction pass. It is not evidence that an external/separate OMNI runtime was invoked.

## Gate criteria
Story-specificity · authored humanity · narrative gain · restraint · authenticity · Aromia identity · mobile independence.

## Ambroxan
**PASS**

Strongest idea: visual invisibility becoming material. The composition is recognizably about this story even without its title.

Refinement accepted: reject literal ice/crystal depiction as factual ambroxan. Any generated translucent matter is interpretive and must not be captioned as the molecule itself. Clary sage is the documentary pivot.

Risk: sterile cosmetic minimalism. Counter by retaining imperfect water/material behavior and botanical specificity.

## Dominique Ropion
**PASS**

Strongest idea: the page itself performs overdose and then subtraction. This creates narrative gain beyond illustration.

Refinement accepted: saturated floral density occurs once as a signature act. Continuous burgundy luxury treatment is rejected. The white/absence passage is mandatory.

Risk: generic niche-perfume campaign. Counter through typographic chronology, asymmetry and absence rather than beauty-product staging.

## Amouage
**PASS_WITH_REFINEMENT**

Strongest idea: arrival → territory → resin → commission → legacy.

Refinement required: the prior moodboard leaned too easily toward generic desert/gold/ornament cues. Final asset selection must privilege Omani-specific documentary truth — frankincense, architecture, landscape/material culture — and treat decorative luxury imagery as suspect.

Risk: Orientalist shorthand. Any asset that could equally advertise an unrelated Gulf luxury hotel is rejected.

## Batch coherence
**PASS**

The three stories share editorial relationships rather than layout:
- warm paper / disciplined type;
- asymmetric image-text tension;
- captions as evidence;
- one dominant visual act per story;
- controlled commerce late in the reading path;
- mobile reduction by editorial priority, not scaling.

They deliberately diverge in atmosphere:
- Ambroxan: air / translucency / mineral light;
- Ropion: compression / floral pressure / release;
- Amouage: threshold / limestone / resin / smoke.

## Anti-template test
Could the exact Ambroxan composition be reused for Ropion? No.
Could Ropion's density-release behavior be reused unchanged for Amouage? No.
Could Amouage's travel/territory sequence be reused for Ambroxan? No.

Result: the system is sufficiently related without becoming a template.

## Asset gate
Proceed, but only assets meeting the following survive:
- generated = interpretive, never documentary;
- people = authentic/licensable or omitted;
- recognizable products = authentic packshots;
- documentary materials/places = sourced and accurate;
- no asset is included merely to fill a visual slot.
