# AROMIA — EDITORIAL ARCHITECTURE v1

STATUS: APPROVED_DIRECTION
PRODUCTION: PENDING

## Principle

Aromia is organized by territories, but it is remembered through stories.

> Las secciones organizan Aromia. Las historias le dan personalidad.

The navigation must clarify the publication without turning the Home into a category grid. Home is a living cover: it answers **¿qué está pensando Aromia ahora?**

## Primary territories

### Historias
Narrative core: stories behind fragrances, houses, cultural moments, commissions, accidents, rituals and events worth telling. Not academic history and not brand chronology.

### Perfumes
One fragrance as editorial protagonist. A perfume appears because there is a reason to devote a story to it, never because the old catalog needs coverage.

### Materia
Ingredients, molecules and physical origins of smell: ambroxan, iris, oud, aldehydes, vetiver, Iso E Super, incense, resins and future material territories. Strong permission for macro, texture, botanical truth and material abstraction.

### Personas
Perfumers, founders, collectors and other people who alter perfume culture. Portraiture is editorial, not celebrity treatment; craft and decisions matter more than biography.

### Saber
Useful knowledge with editorial intelligence: testing, application, concentration, conservation, seasons, olfactory families, developing the nose and buying/exploring with judgment. This absorbs the useful capital of the previous Academia/Guías without becoming a help center.

### Discovery
Self-discovery through perfume: olfactory profiles, sensations, memory, material, context and editorial recommendations. It must not collapse into a conversion quiz.

## Cross-cutting series

### Fuera del radar
Recurring editorial series, not primary navigation. Unknown houses, forgotten perfumes, strange materials and improbable stories. May live inside Historias, Perfumes, Personas or Materia.

### El Coleccionista
Current story and potential recurring series if enough editorial material emerges. Do not create a permanent navigation territory prematurely.

## Archive

`Archivo Aromia` is a future structured layer, initially secondary rather than primary navigation. It should eventually permit exploration by fragrance, house, perfumer, material, year, olfactory family and concept. This is where useful structured knowledge from the former catalog can survive without restoring catalog/store architecture.

## Navigation hypothesis

AROMIA
Historias · Perfumes · Materia · Personas · Saber · Discovery · Buscar

Do not represent these six territories as six mandatory Home cards.

## Home / living cover

The Home is editorially directed and may contain:
- one dominant current story;
- 2–3 counterpoints with intentionally different scale;
- one unexpected route into Discovery;
- a small archive/revisit gesture when inventory supports it;
- episodic visual behavior derived from the lead story.

The cover changes through editorial emphasis, not through a rigid module schedule.

## Commerce

Commerce remains contextual and subordinate. It is not a primary section. Product links may appear only when they extend exploration and never determine editorial conclusions.

## Launch rule

Aromia Editorial v1 does not need equal inventory in every territory. Prefer a directed cover and 6–10 excellent pieces distributed unevenly over artificially filling six categories.

Production cutover requires a coherent publication experience: living cover, representative stories, navigation, mobile behavior, authentic/approved assets, SEO/indexing decisions, contextual commerce, browser QA and final gate.
