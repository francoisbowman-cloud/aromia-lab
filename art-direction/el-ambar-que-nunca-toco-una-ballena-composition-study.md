VISUAL_COMPOSITION: READY

## Narrative behavior
The page begins almost weightless and becomes progressively more material. The visual idea is revelation: something smelled everywhere but rarely seen.

## Desktop sequence
1. **Opening / 90vh** — warm paper, narrow territory marker `MATERIA`, large left-aligned title occupying ~55% width. On the right/lower field, one dominant interpretive macro of translucent mineral/waxy matter under lateral daylight. No bottle.
2. **Lead text** — reading column offset left of center; large empty right margin. First paragraphs remain visually quiet.
3. **The whale problem** — no whale hero. A small archival/documentary image may enter only if sourced; otherwise typography carries history.
4. **Botanical pivot** — asymmetric 60/40 spread. Real clary sage image dominates; a restrained factual caption connects sclareol to the synthesis route. This is the page's first obvious green/living material.
5. **Timeline interruption** — `1946 → 1950 → 1977` set as an editorial chronology, not infographic cards. Dates become typographic anchors inside the reading flow.
6. **Invisible modernity** — near-white field with a single verified chemical/name notation and prose. No fantasy molecule rendering.
7. **Molecule 02** — authentic packshot only, isolated and modest. Product appears as cultural evidence: someone decided to let the molecule become the perfume.
8. **Sí, pero** — return to paper and text. No image.
9. **Commerce close** — contextual links after conclusion, visually subordinate.

## Mobile
Title first, then one full-width macro crop. Preserve air by reducing image count, not merely shrinking desktop. Botanical pivot becomes image → caption → text. Timeline becomes three vertically separated dates. Product packshot max ~55vw and never full-bleed.
