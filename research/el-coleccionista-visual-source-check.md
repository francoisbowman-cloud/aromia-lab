## Rights / authenticity boundary

Official-site availability verifies identity and current lineup; it does not by itself establish a reusable image license for Aromia. Before publishing copied packshots, Code must confirm the project's acceptable rights/source policy or use a permitted product-image source. If that cannot be established, use a factual typographic lineage instead of copying or generating branded bottle approximations.

No generated image may be used as documentary representation of a Jean Paul Gaultier bottle.
