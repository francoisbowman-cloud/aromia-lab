# El Coleccionista — Early OMNI Review

## Result

`EARLY_OMNI: PASS`

This PASS applies to the refined art-direction system and composition study after factual-risk cleanup. It does not authorize publication and is not final browser QA.

## Evidence

### Story specificity — PASS
The visual logic is inseparable from the argument: recognition begins quietly, accumulation progressively occupies the page, the preservation passage changes temporal/material register, and `Sí, pero` releases the density. The composition would lose its meaning if transplanted unchanged to an unrelated article.

### Professional editorial craft — PASS
The system uses controlled reading measure, rail/reading/marginal relationships, progressive density, serial rhythm, deliberate overflow, surface change and a substantial whitespace reset. Hierarchy is carried by proportion, rhythm, placement and typographic behavior rather than decorative effects.

### Authored humanity — PASS
Humanity comes from judgment and restraint: one documentary opening image, no decorative pseudo-handwriting, no fake scrapbook devices, and a deliberate decision to leave Act IV visually empty. The `A.` mark may remain typographic or be omitted; a simulated handwritten signature is not authorized.

### Anti-AI character — PASS
The direction avoids generic luxury signals, compulsory cinematic hero treatment, card grids, glass effects, ornamental ingredients and invented perfume imagery. Repetition has narrative purpose rather than functioning as a reusable visual trick.

### Narrative gain — PASS
Visual density communicates the article's central proposition before and alongside the prose. The transition from proliferation to preservation and then to release adds meaning rather than merely illustrating paragraphs.

### Restraint — PASS
The article is intentionally image-light. The flanker field is typographic/documentary rather than a beauty-shot lineup. Act III does not fabricate a conservation triptych. Act IV uses absence as content.

### Authenticity — PASS WITH IMPLEMENTATION CONSTRAINTS
Factual cleanup is part of the accepted direction: weak `4/10 households` material is not a design dependency; the 30–40% flanker range must remain attributed rather than presented as a universal industry statistic; documentary-looking branded products must use authentic/verifiable material; no invented batch, formula, historical packaging or endorsement evidence is allowed.

### Aromia identity — PASS
The strongest reusable identity is relational rather than templated: typography roles, reading measures, rail/field/marginal logic, captions/credits, whitespace discipline and responsive transformation. The exact accumulation curve, flanker bands, vellum/time episode and `Sí, pero` reset remain story-specific.

## Promotion decision

### Candidate `AROMIA_FOUNDATION`
- typography role relationships, not this article's exact sizes;
- reading measure and disciplined wide/narrow field behavior;
- caption/credit language;
- neutral paper/ink behavior with episodic color derived from real material;
- responsive principle that marginal information becomes meaningful inline/interlude content rather than disappearing;
- accessibility/contrast rule forbidding a weak third gray.

### Candidate `REUSABLE_PRIMITIVE`
- editorial rail / reading field / marginal field;
- marginal note → inline interlude transformation;
- serial/repetition field;
- density transition;
- archive/time field;
- whitespace pause/reset;
- documentary opening image with caption/credit;
- contextual editorial-commerce close.

### Keep `STORY_SPECIFIC`
- the exact 110→56→36→22px accumulation rhythm;
- four flanker bands and right-edge escape;
- the particular vellum treatment for preservation;
- `2010 → hoy` as the temporal gesture;
- 300px release around `Sí, pero`;
- title becoming smaller than body in the reset;
- the exact placement/count of article-specific marginalia.

### Reject / do not canonize
- Canva as primary canvas;
- simulated handwritten signature;
- invented documentary triptych;
- universal use of the three-zone row as a fixed article template;
- product-family beauty shots as default representation of proliferation.
