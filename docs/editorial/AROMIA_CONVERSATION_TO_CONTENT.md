# AROMIA — CONVERSATION TO CONTENT

STATUS: ACTIVE_METHOD
OWNER_INPUT: Publisher / Editor-in-Chief
EDITORIAL_TRANSFORMATION: ChatGPT + Editorial Desk

## Purpose

The Publisher does not need to arrive with an article, brief or polished thesis. Conversation itself may be source material.

> No transcribir la voz. Aprender de ella.

The goal is to preserve the human logic of the Publisher's thinking while transforming it into publishable Aromia editorial material without reproducing accidental speech patterns, filler, repetition or unfinished phrasing.

## Intake

A conversation may begin from:
- an observation;
- a fragrance experience;
- a memory;
- a disagreement with perfume culture or industry behavior;
- a question;
- a contradiction;
- an unfinished intuition;
- an object, material, person or story that provokes curiosity.

No special command such as “this is for Aromia” is required inside the Aromia project. Potential material may be identified without interrupting the conversation.

## Extraction

When enough substance exists, extract a private/editorial Content Guide containing:

1. **Origin** — what triggered the conversation.
2. **Human thesis** — the strongest idea actually expressed or discovered.
3. **Tension** — contradiction, doubt, discomfort or unresolved question that gives the piece energy.
4. **Publisher fingerprints** — memorable phrasing, reasoning habits or observations worth preserving.
5. **Editorial angle** — what Aromia can investigate rather than merely repeat.
6. **Research boundary** — which statements are personal experience/opinion and which require external verification.
7. **Best territory** — Historias / Perfumes / Materia / Personas / Saber / Discovery.
8. **Possible form** — essay, reported story, guide, review, conversation-derived column, short note, series candidate, etc.
9. **Visual seed** — any material, scene, gesture or contrast already implicit in the conversation.
10. **Decision** — DEVELOP / HOLD / DISCARD.

Not every interesting exchange becomes an article.

## Transformation

CONVERSATION
→ SIGNAL EXTRACTION
→ CONTENT GUIDE
→ RESEARCH / FACT-CHECK WHEN NEEDED
→ EDITORIAL DRAFT
→ VOICE REVIEW
→ ART DIRECTION
→ NORMAL AROMIA PRODUCTION PIPELINE

Opinions remain opinions. Memories remain attributed/personal unless independently verified. Added factual claims require support according to Aromia research standards.

## Voice learning

The Voice Bible is a living editorial contract, not a transcript model.

Candidate voice learning is accepted only when a pattern is:
- repeated across meaningful conversations;
- compatible with Aromia's editorial character;
- useful in written form;
- distinguishable from filler, speech error or temporary mood;
- not so specific that it turns every writer/article into an imitation of the Publisher.

### Preserve
- reasoning movement;
- degree of curiosity;
- willingness to question an initial belief;
- specific observations;
- natural contradiction;
- proportion between sophistication and plain speech;
- humor or skepticism when genuinely present;
- ways of arriving at conclusions rather than announcing them.

### Do not preserve mechanically
- filler words;
- accidental repetition;
- incomplete spoken syntax;
- transcription noise;
- habitual typos;
- every colloquialism;
- momentary verbal tics.

## Voice Review cadence

Do not rewrite `AROMIA_VOICE_BIBLE.md` after every conversation. Accumulate evidence in a Voice Learning Log. Promote changes only during a Voice Review after enough evidence exists.

A Voice Review asks:
1. Is this genuinely recurrent?
2. Does it make Aromia more recognizable rather than merely more personal?
3. Does it improve the reader experience?
4. Can other Aromia writers/actors use it without impersonating the Publisher?
5. Does it reduce AI-like generic prose?

Only accepted findings enter the canonical Voice Bible.

## Provenance

Internal origin may be recorded as:
- `AROMIA_DESK` — actor/research-originated editorial idea;
- `FROM_CONVERSATION` — originated materially in Publisher conversation;
- `HYBRID` — conversation seed substantially expanded through independent research/editorial development.

These are internal provenance labels. They are not mandatory public-facing categories.

## Editorial test

A conversation-derived piece should ultimately feel like:

> alguien está pensando conmigo

not:

> alguien transcribió una conversación

and not:

> una IA convirtió notas en contenido.
