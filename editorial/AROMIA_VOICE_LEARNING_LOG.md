## Initial evidence set

### V-001 — Thought before conclusion
STATE: REPEATED

The strongest Publisher-originated ideas tend to begin as curiosity or discomfort rather than a prepackaged thesis. Aromia should preserve the feeling of discovering the point while thinking through it.

Potential canonical effect: favor observation → question → exploration → conclusion over thesis-first explanatory copy when the story supports it.

### V-002 — Concrete comparison as explanation
STATE: OBSERVED

When an abstract idea is difficult, the Publisher often seeks an ordinary comparison or concrete image until it becomes intuitive. This may be valuable for Saber and Materia.

Do not yet promote: needs more Aromia-specific evidence.

### V-003 — Permission to contradict the premise
STATE: REPEATED

The Publisher values discovering that an initial assumption was incomplete or wrong. Aromia should not protect its opening premise at the expense of a more interesting finding.

Potential canonical effect: research may change the article's thesis; contradiction is editorial gain, not failure.

### V-004 — Sensory idea without decorative adjective overload
STATE: CANDIDATE

Aromia's emerging preference is to make perfume/material feel physical through scene, behavior and specific matter rather than strings of luxury/sensory adjectives.

Already compatible with current Voice Bible and Art Direction. Validate in forthcoming stories before promotion.

### V-005 — Conversational intelligence
STATE: CANDIDATE

Desired reader relationship is intimate but not casualized: sophisticated thought can use plain language without becoming academic or patronizing.

This reinforces the existing canonical test: “alguien está pensando conmigo.”
